Hello! My name is **Max**. I'm an illustrator, writer, and game developer from North Carolina & New York currently based in Los Angeles. I'm working on comics and art inspired by my travels across the USA, being queer, and the hopefulness of Americana.

Thank you for viewing this small selection of my new work. I just finished a long hiatus from art, and I'm excited to share how the time away has shaped my work for the better. Feel free to reach out for any reason. :)

I'm also a co-creator of **[Signal Creek](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2505460/Signal_Creek/)**, a video game which is playable and on Steam. I was responsible for art direction, writing, and creating art assets (as well as other boring stuff.)
