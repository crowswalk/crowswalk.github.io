Hello! My name is **Max**. I'm an illustrator, writer, and game developer from North Carolina & New York currently based in Los Angeles. I'm working on comics and art inspired by my travels across the USA, being queer, and the hopefulness of Americana.

Thank you for viewing this small selection of my new work. I'm just coming out of a long hiatus from illustration but working hard to expand my portfolio. Though it was difficult, my time away from drawing has made my voice stronger and I'm exicted to share it with people! Feel free to reach out for any reason. :)

I'm also a co-creator of **[snacktime games](http://snacktimegamestudio.com)**! Our game Signal Creek is on Steam. I was responsible for art direction, writing, and creating art assets (as well as other boring stuff.)
